FAQ

Common questions.

Is DIVIDND really free?+
Yes — our free plan is genuinely free, no credit card required. You get 1 portfolio with up to 5 positions and all core features. Premium unlocks unlimited portfolios and positions for $59/year.
How many portfolios can I create?+
Free plan: 1 portfolio with up to 5 positions. Premium: unlimited portfolios and positions. Switch between them instantly from the top bar.
Can I cancel my subscription?+
Yes, anytime from your account settings with one click. No questions asked. Your data is always yours and always exportable.
What happens if I downgrade?+
Your data stays safe. You can still view existing portfolios and positions, but won't be able to add new portfolios or exceed 5 positions until you upgrade again.
How is my data protected?+
Your portfolio data is protected by Row Level Security at the database level. Only your authenticated account can read or write your data — not other users, not us.
Where do the stock quotes come from?+
Quotes are sourced from Alpha Vantage, which provides real-time and delayed market data for thousands of stocks and ETFs on major global exchanges.
Can I track ETFs, mutual funds, and Canadian stocks?+
Yes. Any ticker symbol on a major exchange works — US stocks, Canadian stocks (TSX), ETFs, and most mutual funds. Just enter the symbol exactly as it trades.
Can I export my data?+
Yes. Export any portfolio as a JSON file at any time. Your data is yours — always portable, never locked in.
